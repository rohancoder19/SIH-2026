# SurakshitSthan AI — Multi-Hazard ML & Explainable AI Methodology

## 1. Hybrid Risk Architecture

SurakshitSthan AI separates numerical computation from natural language reasoning:

1. **Deterministic Mathematical Risk Engine**:
   - Multi-hazard risk score calculated strictly via weighted formulas & Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classification.
   - Features: Rainfall saturation (31%), Geotechnical slope (24%), Seismic history (21%), River proximity (14%), Topographic elevation (10%).

2. **Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution**:
   - Computes statistical feature contributions for every habitation risk score.
   - Highlights primary hazard drivers and exact influence percentages.

3. **Google Gemini LLM Briefings**:
   - Gemini models (Gemini 2.5 Flash) strictly ingest calculated numerical scores to produce executive NDMA evacuation briefs and actionable step-by-step guidance.
